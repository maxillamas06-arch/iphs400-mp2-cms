# Client brief — Club de Aficionados Albiazul

Unofficial Rayados (C.F. Monterrey) fan club. Not affiliated with the club. All names and content in this project are made up.

## Who runs the site
One fan club leader. He got the job of being the admin for the whole thing. He knows the basics on a computer, nothing technical, so the admin side has to be simple.

## Who writes
The members. Basically any fan who wants to share their opinion about the team: how a match went, what they think of the lineup, the coach, a signing.

## Who reads it, and what they want
Other fans. They mostly want to express themselves and see what other people think. It's about opinions, not official news.

## What's broken today
Right now people post their opinions on X or Facebook. It works, but it's all mixed with everything else. I think a site just for this, just for Rayados fans, is better.

## What would make it better
One place where the fan opinions live, organized, and easy to read.

## What must never happen
No aggression toward other people. No threats against people or places. Trash talk toward other teams (tiraderas to Tigres, etc.) is fine, that's part of being a fan, but it can never cross into threats or attacking someone personally. That's why nothing should go public without the leader checking it first.
